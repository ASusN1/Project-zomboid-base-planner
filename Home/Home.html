About the Project

Project Zomboid base Planner is a fan made project, created for the purpose of helping players plan and design their base or any kind of building actually build them.

Thanks to The Indie Stone for creating Project Zomboid https://projectzomboid.com/), which made this possible. This is an unofficial fan production for non-commercial purposes made under the Indie Stone Terms [link to these terms].

Documentation

Contain tutorial how to use this web app and other stuff

TO place object , choose the place object button or press "p" for place object. To delete object, choose the delete button or press "backspace" the hover to the object you want delete and click on it. To rotate object, choose the rotate button or press "r" and hover to the object you want to rotate and click on it. To change the directiom of wall placement, press on wall placement direction to change between N, E, S, W direction. To save your design online, press save design online or use the follolwing short cut: ctrol + s. TO capture your design an export it as a picture, press export design as picture. To access your saved design online, press on the "Your Design" button on the left side bar, then click on the design you want to edit To select object and place , simply press the object category, this will show you a list of object belong to that category with their sprite To share your design with other plaer, press "share design to community button" , this will make a copy version of your design and therefore other player can view it under community design. to view other player design, press community design, it will show you a list of design shared by other player, click on the design you want to view and it will show you a preview of that design. This preview is interable, as you able to move around the design and view the object surrdouning it ( in the future update,I will add a hide btn to hide object and a show btn to show all hidden object ) To sign up or log in, press the sign up or log in button on the top right corner, this will show you a pop up window to sign up or log in. If you already have an account, you can log in with your email and password, if you don't have an account, you can sign up with your email and password. (The ability to sign in with discord/slack will be update in the near future) You permenantly delete your base design my simply press delete card btn, you will have to confirm the delete to delete, you can cancel it if you want to make a copy verion of a design yuu want to make a copy off from community design, simplky press ciopy deisng btn, this will show you the card deisn g, remember, you have to press saved base design online to save it online If there are any probems or bug , please report it to me via discord or email, I will try to fix it as soon as possible. for feature request, please send a message to me via discord or email, I will try to implement it as soon as possible.
