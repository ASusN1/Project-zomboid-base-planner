Welcome to Zomboid Base Builder

This is a tool designed to help you plan and design your base in the game Project Zomboid. With an isometric grid, you can easily visualize and layout your base, ensuring that you make the most of your space and resources.

Use the builder to experiment with different layouts, test various building materials, and create a safe haven for your survivors. Whether you're a seasoned player or new to the game, this tool will assist you in creating the perfect base to withstand the zombie apocalypse.
