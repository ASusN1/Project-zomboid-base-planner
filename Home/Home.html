About the Project

Project Zomboid base Planner is a fan made project, created for the purpose of helping players plan and design their base or any kind of building actually build them.

Thanks to The Indie Stone for creating Project Zomboid https://projectzomboid.com/), which made this possible. This is an unofficial fan production for non-commercial purposes made under the Indie Stone Terms [link to these terms].

Documentation

Contain tutorial how to use this web app and other stuff

Learn how to use the web app, manage your designs, and share your design with other players.

1. Baisc Control and Shortcuts

  • Place Objects: You can place objects(such as walls, tiles, and crates, and much more) by pressing the button or press P
  • Delete Objects: You can delete objects by pressing the button or press backspace
  • Rotate Objects: You can rotate objects by pressing the button or press R
  • Wall Direction: Press Wall Placement Direction to change between N, E, S, W.

2. Create Your Designs

  • Save Designs: You can save your designs by clicking the Save base design btn button.
  • Export Design as image: Press Export Design as Pictureto capture and download your design.

3. Community & Sharing

  • Share Designs: You can share your design to the community by clicking the Share Design to Community button.
  • Unshare Designs: You can unshare your design by clicking the Unshare Design button(only viewable if you shared the base design).
  • View Community Designs: Click the Community Design button on the sidebar to view designs shared by other players.
  • Copy Community Designs: You can make a copy of a community design by clicking the Make a copy of the design button in the community design preview.

4. Account & Support

ClickSign Up/Log In at the top right conner to sign up or log in or create an account with your email.

Note: Discord and Slack sign-in option will be added in the future update

If there are any problems or bugs, or if you have feature requests, please report them via Discord or github Email
